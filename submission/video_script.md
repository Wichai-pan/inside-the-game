## Choices and cautions

- Every on-screen agent line was checked against the event data before choosing the moment. Avoid in re-takes: the 3:11 analyst line ("Callum Ashby finishes Callum Ashby's shot…" for a blocked shot) and the 6:15 English analyst line (credits Brennan with Kendrick's pass).
- The 2:43 goal's analyst line needed 3 attempts (37 s) and is dropped because play moves on; it is not a good on-screen rejection example. The 3:39 casual line (scene 8) is.
- `ask.py` answers are not fact-checked. An earlier run of "Why did Ridgeway win?" called 0.31 xG "superior" to 1.04; only use outputs you have checked.
- Do not say Foundry or Azure is used live; "ready for Microsoft Foundry" is accurate (provider switch exists, not run).
- A human voice-over would present better than the synthetic voice; keep the timings and it stays under 2:00.
