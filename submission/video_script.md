English voice-over; on-screen text in English. Chinese appears only as product output, with an English caption saying what it is.

Recording setup: desktop browser at 1440×900 (or 1920×1080), page zoom 100%, Match 42, playback at 4×. Record the public site https://wichai-pan.github.io/inside-the-game/ after the final page design is published.

## Notes for recording

- Word count of the voice-over is about 260 words, which fits 1:48 at a calm pace (~150 words per minute). If a take runs long, cut the second sentence of shot 4 and shot 8.
- Scene 7 should show the rejected-line loop. If the page does not animate it, show a rejected line from a generation log (`insidegame.live` prints attempts per line) or the `retried_lines` count from the bundle.
- Do not say "Foundry" or "Azure" as if used live. If mentioned, say: "The model client is swappable; it runs on a local model today and can switch to Microsoft Foundry."
- Captions: burn in subtitles of the voice-over, or upload an SRT to YouTube, for viewers without sound.
- Upload to YouTube as **Public** or **Unlisted** (the rules ask for a public URL; Public is safest). Title: "Inside the Game – Microsoft x Premier League Hackathon demo".
