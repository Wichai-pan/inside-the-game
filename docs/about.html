How it works

A broadcast team of agents, checked against the data.

Inside the Game reads a football match feed the way a broadcast crew does: it measures what happened, decides which moments are worth talking about, explains why, and says it in the voice each viewer wants. Nothing reaches the screen until it has been checked against the feed.

Watch a match ▶Open the post-match studio

One match, four broadcasts

The same events produce a different commentary for each viewer. Switch between them live in the match centre.

CASUAL FAN

Plain words, the story first

Who has the upper hand, why a moment mattered, few numbers. Big captions and a simple possession bar.

ANALYST

Metrics, reasons and evidence

Pass difficulty, ball and shot speed, xG, field position, the event IDs behind every claim, and live team stats.

ENGLISH · 中文 · YOUR PLAYER

Your language, your focus

Each line is written natively in English or Simplified Chinese. Follow a club or a single player to see their touches, sprints and involvement.

The live workflow

A Microsoft Agent Framework workflow with typed executors. Blue steps call a language model; the rest are deterministic code.

CodeFeed ingestor

Events arrive in time order with tracking positions.

CodeAnalyst

Pass difficulty, speeds, xG, possession; ranks the moments that matter and why.

AgentNarrator

Writes one line per viewer type and language from the facts only.

CodeFact checker

Rejects names, numbers or actions that are not in the data.

AgentClaim auditor

Extracts who shot, who won the ball, who leads and where; code compares with the feed.

CodeProducer

Times the line into the broadcast next to instant play-by-play.

↺ Retry loop: a rejected line goes back to the narrator with the reason. After three failures a template line is used, and the page says so.

THE DATA

Synthetic matches, real movement

  • Live-ball spells are cut from the DFL open data (Bassek et al. 2025, CC BY 4.0).
  • A seeded generator stitches spells from several matches into a new match between two fictional clubs, Harbor City and Ridgeway United.
  • Real players are mapped to fictional players by position; every bundle records which source spell each part came from.
LIMITS WE SHOW, NOT HIDE

Honest about what is checked

  • Every commentary line says whether an agent wrote it, how many attempts it took, or whether a template replaced it.
  • The analyst view shows the measured delay of each line and which ones were dropped because play had moved on.
  • Checks cover names, numbers, measurements, the shooter, the ball winner, the leader and the half; other errors can still get through.
